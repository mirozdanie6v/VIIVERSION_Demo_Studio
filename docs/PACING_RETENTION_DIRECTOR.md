# Pacing & Retention Director

## Purpose

Demo Studio product videos must feel like persuasive product storytelling, not a complete screen recording.

The edit exists to make the viewer:
1. understand value quickly;
2. keep receiving useful answers;
3. want to see the next proof;
4. reach a strong conversion CTA.

## Core narrative unit

Every meaningful beat follows:

**Question / friction → visible solution → proof → customer benefit**

Examples:

- “Как быстро найти подходящий тур?” → show catalog/filter → show result → “выбор занимает меньше времени”.
- “Нужно ли писать менеджеру за каждой деталью?” → open tour card → show program/price → “всё уже собрано в одном месте”.
- “Что если у клиента вопрос?” → ask AI → show answer → “клиент получает помощь сразу”.
- “Что получает менеджер?” → show structured booking → “меньше хаотичных сообщений, более готовая заявка”.

Do not narrate interface anatomy for its own sake.

## Hook

First 3–5 seconds must contain:
- product identity;
- concrete business/customer promise;
- immediate visible proof or movement.

Avoid a slow logo-only opening.

Example structure:
“Туры, ответы и бронирование — в одном мобильном пути.”
Then immediately show the product doing it.

## Voice-led timeline

Narration is the master rhythm.

- Preserve Niki/approved voice performance.
- Remove dead gaps between spoken blocks unless a visual proof genuinely needs recognition time.
- Typical silence between narrated ideas: 0.15–0.55 s.
- Longer pause (0.7–1.2 s) only for a major reveal/result.
- Never keep browser-capture waiting time merely because it existed in source.

## Visual beat duration

A narrated sentence may continue across multiple visual beats.

Targets:
- visual micro-beat: 1.2–2.8 s;
- normal focus beat: 2.0–3.5 s;
- important proof/result: 2.5–4.5 s;
- full static hold above 4.5 s requires explicit editorial justification.

If narration lasts 10–13 seconds, use 3–5 meaningful visual beats under the same continuous voice.

Do **not** treat “complete spoken thought” as permission for one 13-second static shot.

## Pattern of visual change

Useful changes include:
- establish → semantic crop;
- scroll to a new information block;
- target shift within the same screen;
- click/action;
- result state;
- detail insert;
- release to context;
- short graphic/value callout;
- controlled transition to next step.

Movement must add meaning. Random motion is prohibited.

## Retention rhythm

The viewer should receive a new piece of information, proof or visual emphasis approximately every 1.5–3.5 seconds.

Avoid repetitive cadence. Alternate:
- wide/context;
- medium semantic focus;
- close detail;
- action/result.

Do not repeat identical zoom strength and duration on every beat.

## Benefit density

At least every 8–12 seconds, the narration must state or strongly imply a customer/business benefit.

The viewer should never spend a long section only learning “where buttons are”.

Preferred language:
- faster choice;
- fewer questions to manager;
- current price/availability;
- clearer booking;
- instant AI answer;
- structured lead;
- less lost demand;
- smoother customer journey.

## Compression rules

Remove or aggressively shorten:
- redundant page orientation;
- loading/waiting;
- repeated full-screen holds;
- repeated explanations of obvious UI;
- scroll travel that contains no useful information;
- duplicate proof;
- closing recap.

Current MAX TOUR benchmark:
- approved Niki voice: ~106.6 s;
- previous visual runtime: ~199.1 s;
- target next edit: approximately 115–130 s without changing the approved voice.

## CTA

The final 5–8 seconds must **sell the next action**, not merely show a logo.

CTA sequence:
1. strongest final benefit/proof;
2. direct proposition;
3. immediate action.

Example:
“Хотите такой же клиентский путь для своих туров?”
“VIIVERSION адаптирует систему под ваш бизнес.”
“Напишите нам — покажем ваш вариант.”

CTA visual:
- premium final product/device state;
- strong headline;
- one clear action;
- brand signature;
- no long fade-out after the ask.

## Audio rhythm

- Music supports pace and emotional lift.
- Add subtle transition accents only when they reinforce a semantic/action beat.
- No constant overactive SFX.
- Music may lift slightly on hook, key proof and CTA.
- Voice always remains dominant.

## Critic gates

Warn when:
- average visual scene > 7 s;
- median visual scene > 6 s;
- any speech scene > 6 s without internal visual beats;
- static hold > 4.5 s;
- dead silence between narrated blocks > 1.2 s without proof reason;
- no explicit customer benefit for >12 s;
- first strong value proposition arrives after 5 s;
- CTA begins only after the useful content has visibly ended.

Reject when:
- more than 25% of runtime is non-semantic waiting;
- final CTA is missing;
- a 10+ second narration block is represented by one unchanged framing.
